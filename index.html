    How the auction works

    1. i.

      Bid against the room

      One lot at a time, the price climbs. Raise the bid or drop out. Whoever makes the last bid wins the lot.

    2. ii.

      Pay less than it's worth

      When the hammer falls, the real price is revealed. Win below it to score, with a bonus for landing within 5%. Overpay a little and you lose a little; overpay a lot and it hurts. Even if you lose, a top bid close to the real price earns points.

    3. iii.

      Beat the room

      You're ranked against today's three rivals. Same lots and same rivals for everyone, with a new sale at midnight.

    Meet the room
